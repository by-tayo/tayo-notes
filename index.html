Where am I right now?

Currently, my life has been filled with adventures, both emotionally and through exploring new places.

After seven years of being single, since high school, I experienced a four-month relationship. It taught me what being in a relationship could feel like: going places together, getting to know someone, and sharing experiences. Over time, we drifted apart because we wanted different things, and distance and difficult decisions became part of the experience.

I’m now in my sixth internship, working with a state government agency. Before this, I interned at a well-known fintech company in Austin. Austin is a beautiful place to explore, from its shopping destinations to its vibrant technology scene and the opportunities it offers.

My current internship has given me more experience in cybersecurity and a better understanding of the field, from SOC analysts responding to incidents under pressure to security engineers designing and strengthening digital infrastructure.

I’m approaching a major milestone: graduating with my BBA in Cybersecurity in December 2026. I imagine life after graduation feeling a little like the summer days of high school, when there was no school to worry about. I look forward to having more freedom to enjoy life and experience the world around me.

02

What interests me?

  • Technology and cybersecurity
  • Finance and business risk
  • Personal stories and travel
  • Learning Japanese and Korean
  • Creative writing and website design
03

What do I want to build?

I envision creating a personal blog where I can share what I’ve learned, document my experiences, and connect with others exploring similar paths.

I imagine a paper-inspired website with neutral colors, meaningful symbols, and subtle Japanese and Korean typography. I want my name represented in these languages as part of the aesthetic, bringing together my interests, personality, and creativity.

More than anything, I want this website to be a place where I can share knowledge while continuing to learn from others.

04

One thing to remember

I hope my future self remembers this moment and understands that life is a marathon.

The distance between where I am and where I want to be isn’t covered all at once. It happens through small steps, consistent effort, and patience.

Life isn’t a race. It’s not about how you start it, but how you finish it.
05

One thing I want to do tomorrow

Play video games and enjoy life.

Not everything has to be productive. Sometimes, it’s enough to relax, have fun, and enjoy the moment.

A reminder to myself

I am allowed to build my future while enjoying the present moment.

Finding My Way Back to Myself

On rest, trust, and building a future without losing myself.

01

How am I feeling today?

Today, I feel somewhat happy, somewhat sad, and low on energy. I can’t name exactly what I’m feeling. I think it’s a mixture of emotions as I try to manage everything going on in my life.

One thing that has been on my mind lately is how important financial decisions have become. The choices I make today can shape my future, so I want to be more thoughtful about how I manage my money and plan for the life I want.

02

What did I experience today?

I’m preparing to transition into the vulnerability management area with the tunneling team. I also recently interviewed with a higher education institution for a cybersecurity co-op.

During the interview, I noticed that many of the questions focused on software development, DevOps, and cloud technologies. These are areas I’m familiar with, and the conversation reminded me of the technical research I’ve done in the past.

It felt good to revisit those subjects. Sometimes an opportunity reconnects me with an interest I stepped away from for a while. Whether I’m reconnecting with someone from my past or sitting in an interview, moments like these remind me that I can return to something after a hiatus and find my way forward again.

03

What kind of friendships do I want to build?

I want to build friendships with people who understand that I have flaws, just as they do. I value honesty because the truth helps me understand who I am and where I might need to change.

I want friendships where we can be honest with each other without tearing one another down. I want to be able to accept constructive feedback, recognize my mistakes, and grow into a better version of myself.

I also understand that building friendships takes effort. I have to approach people and give relationships a chance to develop, even when it feels uncomfortable.

04

What am I learning about myself?

My past experiences have shaped how I approach trust. They’ve taught me to be cautious about letting people get close. They’ve also taught me to notice when someone crosses my boundaries or turns a small disagreement into something bigger.

At the same time, I know I can’t build meaningful friendships if I never give people a chance. Everyone has their own responsibilities, and people get busy. Not everyone will connect with me, and that’s okay.

I’m learning that I can protect my boundaries without closing myself off completely.

I’m also learning that it’s important to balance ambition with enjoying life. I want to work toward my goals, but I also need time to relax, decompress, and enjoy the things that make me who I am.

05

What do I want to remember?

I want to remember that I need to slow down sometimes.

Taking breaks, stepping away from my screen, and giving myself time to decompress are important parts of taking care of myself. I don’t want to become so focused on my responsibilities that I forget to protect my peace of mind.

Rest is not wasted time.

Sometimes, stepping away gives me the space I need to come back with a clearer head.

06

What am I looking forward to?

I want to enjoy the little things in life.

I’d like to explore new stores, discover new places to eat, and spend time playing video games. These activities might seem simple, but they give me something to look forward to outside of school, work, and career planning.

I want to remind myself that I don’t have to accomplish something significant every day for the day to be worthwhile.

07

What does it mean to become anything in this world while remaining humble?

To me, it means respecting people from different backgrounds and recognizing that everyone has something to teach me.

I never know how a simple act of kindness or respect might affect someone, or how our paths might cross again in the future. Being open to other people’s experiences lets me learn about them and understand perspectives beyond my own.

I want to keep growing, stay ambitious, and recognize my own worth without believing I’m above anyone else.

My closing thought

I want to keep building my future without losing myself in the process. I can pursue my career, make better financial decisions, develop meaningful friendships, and still give myself permission to rest and enjoy life.

I don’t have to choose between becoming successful and being at peace with who I am today.
